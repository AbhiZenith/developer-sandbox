Paneer Tikka Masala

Home

Description

Paneer Tikka Masala is a rich, comforting North Indian curry featuring Indian cottage cheese (paneer) cooked in a fragrant tomato-cream gravy. The paneer cubes are lightly marinated in seasoned yogurt and pan-seared before being simmered in a gently spiced sauce. It offers a smooth texture with balanced, savory flavors that pair exceptionally well with warm naan or steamed Basmati rice.

This dish is a popular highlight of Indian cuisine around the world because of its rich depth of flavor without being overly fiery. By searing the marinated paneer beforehand, the cheese absorbs the smoky notes of the marinade while keeping its softinterior intact as it thickens inside the gravy.

Ingredients

  • 200g of cubed paneer
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or ghee
  • 1 chopped onion, 2 pureed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • salt to taste
  • fresh coriander leaves for garnishing

Steps

  1. Marinate the paneer cubes in yogurt, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and lightly sear the paneer cubes until golden, then set them aside.
  3. In the same pan, sauté the chopped onion and ginger-garlic paste until soft and translucent.
  4. Add the tomato puree, garam masala, chili powder, and salt; cook on medium heat until the oil begins to separate from the sauce.
  5. Stir in the seared paneer, simmer gently for 5 minutes, and garnish with fresh coriander before serving.
