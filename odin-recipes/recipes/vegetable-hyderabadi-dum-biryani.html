Vegetable Hyderabadi Dum Biryani

Home

Description

Vegetable Hyderabadi Dum Biryani is an iconic, aromatic rice dish made by slow-cooking spiced vegetables alongside fragrant Basmati rice. The dish relies on the "dum" cooking technique, where steam is sealed inside the pot to infuse the rice with deep, layered flavors of fresh herbs and whole spices. It is a hearty main course that brings together vibrant textures and fragrant whole spices.

What makes this biryani special is the delicate layering process. Instead of mixing everything together in a wok, par-boiled rice is laid directly over a rich vegetable base, garnished with caramelised fried onions, mint, and cilantro, and then slow-cooked until every grain of rice absorbs the aromatic steam.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups of soaked Basmati rice
  • 1.5 cups of mixed chopped vegetables (carrots, green beans, peas, potatoes)
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 thinly sliced onion
  • 1 tbsp chopped mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • whole spices (1 bay leaf, 2 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick)
  • 1 tsp biryani masala powder
  • salt to taste

Steps

  1. Par-boil the soaked Basmati rice in salted water with the whole spices until it is about 70% cooked, then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a deep pot, fry the sliced onions until golden brown, and remove half of them for garnishing.
  3. Add the mixed vegetables, yogurt, biryani masala, and salt into the pot, cooking until the vegetables are tender.
  4. Layer the par-boiled rice directly over the cooked vegetable mixture, then sprinkle the reserved fried onions, mint, and fresh coriander over the top.
  5. Cover tightly with a lid and cook on very low heat for 15 minutes to allow the steam to finish cooking the rice and meld the flavors.
